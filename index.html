Import a WhatsApp chat

  1. In WhatsApp, open the chat and tap their name at the top.
  2. Scroll down, tap Export Chat, then Without Media.
  3. Choose Save to Files.
  4. Come back here and pick that file.

The chat is read on this phone and never uploaded. Only the last 300 messages are kept.

✨ AI replies

Gemini reads the recent chat, so replies respond to what was actually said. It’s free with a Google account, within daily limits.

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with Google.
  2. Tap Create API key, then copy it.
  3. Paste it below and tap Test.

Privacy: with this on, the recent messages in a chat, your notes about that person and your texting style are sent to Google to write replies. Google may use free-tier data to improve its products. Quick replies and lazy mode never leave your phone. Your key stays on this phone and isn’t included in backups.
